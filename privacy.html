Privacy Policy

Hari Seldon Alerts · effective October 7, 2026

Who this covers

Hari Seldon Alerts is a personal SMS alert service operated by Janette Warner, sole proprietor, Yellow Springs, Ohio. Its only recipient is Janette Warner's own mobile phone.

Information collected

The service stores one phone number, the account owner's own, so it can send her alerts. It collects no information from anyone else.

Sharing

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information is used only to deliver the alerts the owner requested, through the SMS provider Twilio.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies, about 10 messages a month.
