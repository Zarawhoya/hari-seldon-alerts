Hari Seldon Alerts

A private text-message alert service run by Janette Warner, sole proprietor, for her own use. It sends to one phone only: hers.

What it sends

A program on Janette's own computer watches her own brokerage account. When a protective stop order fills, or something needs her decision, it sends her a short text. She may reply with a one-word instruction. There is no marketing, no promotional content, and no third-party recipients.

HARI SELDON: BWET stop filled at $880.82. Reply SELL or HOLD. Reply STOP to end alerts.

Consent and opt-in

The only recipient is the account owner, Janette Warner. She set up this service and registered the sending number herself on October 7, 2026, and she consents to receive these alerts at her own mobile number. No one else is or can be subscribed.
